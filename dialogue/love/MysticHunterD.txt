Narrator: The hunter sits by the fire, struggling to meditate. There has been precious little time to rest. Battle fatigue and corruption are starting to be felt, even for a tenacious young man. 
Hunter: Sweat beads on his brow, and his arms tremble from exhaustion.

Mystic: You are tensing too much, Azun’bir. Meditation can shield the mind and slow the corruption in the body, but it will not wash it out. 
Hunter: As you say, Seeker… but I must do something about these urges or I will not sleep. 
Will you not show me some meditations of your order to help?
Mystic: I could, but they would not help you right now. You are a talented student already.
The older man strokes his beard, gesturing to the hunter’s noticeably bulging pants.
To dispel the excess energy building up, relieving yourself of that swelling would be more efficient.
Hunter: The hunter blushes and shakes his head.
Mistress has allowed me …permission…to, to give myself relief as needed. In order to succeed at this hunt. But I… I am ashamed to say… my arms are too weary for that right now… I can scarcely work them.
